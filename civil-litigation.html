Civil Litigation

Steady, experienced advocacy, from the negotiating table to the courtroom.

SLH

When a dispute can't be resolved on your own, you need an advocate who knows how Oklahoma courts work. Civil litigation has been a cornerstone of Steven L. Hendrickson's practice for over 40 years, spanning contract, property, and general civil matters across more than 30 Oklahoma counties.

He evaluates each case honestly, tells you where you stand, and pursues the most efficient path to a favorable result, whether that means a negotiated settlement or a vigorous trial. The goal is always to protect your interests without unnecessary cost or delay.

How Steven can help

  • Breach of contract disputes
  • Debt collection & creditor matters
  • Real-property & ownership disputes
  • Landlord–tenant matters
  • Business & commercial disputes
  • Small claims
  • Negligence & general civil claims
  • Settlement negotiation & mediation
