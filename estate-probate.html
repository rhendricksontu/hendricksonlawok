Estate Planning & Probate

Protect your family and your wishes, with planning that brings peace of mind.

A thoughtful estate plan is one of the greatest gifts you can leave your family. Steven L. Hendrickson helps Oklahomans put clear, legally sound plans in place, and stands beside families during probate when a loved one has passed.

Whether you are planning ahead or settling an estate, he explains your options in plain language and handles the details with care, so your family is protected and your wishes are honored.
