Steven L. Hendrickson · Attorney at Law

Steven L. Hendrickson has practiced law in Oklahoma since 1981, earning his Juris Doctor from Oklahoma City University. For more than 40 years he has guided clients across Oklahoma City and 30+ counties through some of the most important moments of their lives.

He has also served as the City Judge of Tuttle for over 25 years and is an active member of the Oklahoma Judges Association and the Oklahoma Bar Association: perspective from both sides of the bench that few attorneys can offer.

The result is a practice grounded in experience, integrity, and genuine care for the people he represents.
