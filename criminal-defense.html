Criminal Defense

Protecting your rights, your record, and your future.

A criminal charge can put your freedom, your livelihood, and your reputation at risk. From the moment you're charged, you have rights, and you deserve an experienced attorney who will protect them. Steven L. Hendrickson has defended Oklahomans in felony and misdemeanor matters for more than 40 years.

Having also served as a municipal judge for over 25 years, he understands the courtroom from every angle. He examines the evidence, challenges the prosecution's case, and pursues the best possible outcome, whether that's a dismissal, a reduced charge, or an acquittal at trial.

How Steven can help

  • Felony defense
  • Misdemeanor defense
  • DUI / DWI
  • Drug offenses
  • Traffic & municipal charges
  • Expungements
  • Protective order defense
  • Criminal appeals

If you've been charged, time matters. Contact Hendrickson Law right away for a free, confidential consultation.
