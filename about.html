Experience & Background

More than 40 years of Oklahoma legal experience.

Steven L. Hendrickson has practiced law in Oklahoma since 1981. A graduate of Oklahoma City University School of Law, he has spent more than four decades representing Oklahoma families, individuals, and businesses across Oklahoma City and more than 30 counties statewide.

His practice is broad by design. Over the years he has handled hundreds of matters in family law and divorce, civil litigation, probate and estate matters, criminal defense, personal injury, and real estate, giving clients a single trusted advisor for many of life's most significant legal needs.

What sets Steven apart is perspective. For over 25 years he has served as the City Judge of Tuttle, Oklahoma, and he is an active member of the Oklahoma Judges Association and the Oklahoma Bar Association. Few attorneys can draw on experience from both sides of the bench: insight that benefits his clients every day.

Beyond the Practice

A lifelong Oklahoman, rooted in family and community.

Steven's commitment to people extends well beyond the courtroom. He has been married to his wife, Sherry, since 1982. Together they raised three sons, all now married with families of their own, and Steven is the proud grandfather of seven grandchildren.

He is an active member of Crossings Community Church in Oklahoma City. Outside of work, he spends his time with family and through his church.

That same dedication shows up in how he practices law: with patience, integrity, and a genuine investment in the people he represents. Clients aren't case numbers; they're neighbors.
