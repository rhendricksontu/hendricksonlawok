Personal Injury

Pursuing fair compensation when someone else's negligence causes you harm.

An injury caused by someone else's carelessness can bring medical bills, lost income, and lasting hardship. You shouldn't have to bear that burden alone. Steven L. Hendrickson helps injured Oklahomans hold negligent parties accountable and pursue the compensation they deserve.

He deals with the insurance companies and the legal process so you can focus on recovery, and he's prepared to take your case to trial if a fair settlement isn't offered.

How Steven can help

  • Auto & motor vehicle accidents
  • Slip-and-fall & premises liability
  • Negligence claims
  • Wrongful death
  • Insurance claim disputes
  • Settlement negotiation
